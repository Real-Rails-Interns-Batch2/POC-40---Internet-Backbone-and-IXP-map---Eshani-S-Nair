▸ B   Why This Matters

The internet's physical layer is far more concentrated than it appears. Behind every "cloud" service sits a handful of critical exchange points where thousands of networks physically swap traffic.

A disruption at DE-CIX Frankfurt or AMS-IX Amsterdam can degrade connectivity for hundreds of millions of users across multiple continents. Builders routing around choke points and allocators tracking infrastructure risk need to understand where the real leverage points are — and who controls them.
