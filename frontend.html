▸ B   WHY THIS MATTERS

The internet's physical layer is far more concentrated than it appears. Behind every "cloud" service sits a handful of critical exchange points — massive physical facilities where thousands of networks swap traffic.

A disruption at a single major IXP like DE-CIX Frankfurt or AMS-IX Amsterdam can degrade connectivity for hundreds of millions of users across multiple continents. Builders and allocators need to understand where the real leverage points are — and who controls them.
